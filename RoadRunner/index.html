Operating thesis

Most security tools tell you to be afraid.

RoadRunner tells you what to fix.

NAMES. HOSTNAMES. APP IDS. ATTACK-PATH EDGES. VALIDATION CRITERIA.
The artifact matters because it turns anxiety into a work queue.

Dashboard says“You are at risk.”
RoadRunner saysThese identities, devices, policies, and paths need action this week.
Audit says“Here is a point-in-time report.”
RoadRunner saysHere is the next run condition that proves closure.
Score says“Improve the number.”
RoadRunner saysClose the exposure and show the changed evidence.
The unit of work

This is what a finding looks like.

Not a chart. Not a score. A named exposure with an owner-ready fix and the evidence condition that closes it.

Sample assessment finding · RR-F-0117

Standing privileged access remains assigned outside emergency workflow

HIGH
Affected3 Global Admins, 2 Privileged Role Administrators
EvidenceEntra role assignments observed in current evidence snapshot; no just-in-time activation record attached.
RiskCompromised daily-use or stale privileged accounts can bypass normal access controls and expand blast radius.
Recommended actionMove standing admins to eligible access, require phishing-resistant MFA, and document break-glass exceptions.
ValidationNext run must show no standing privileged assignment except approved break-glass accounts.
Closure rule Manual status cannot close this finding. Evidence from the next run must satisfy the validation condition.
