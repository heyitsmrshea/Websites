Scenario

A client has privilege sprawl, Conditional Access gaps, and on-prem lateral movement exposure.

The walkthrough follows one synthetic weekly run from baseline through findings, remediation queue, validation, and reporting.

01BaselineConnect read-only sources and establish the first evidence snapshot.
02FindingsCreate named findings tied to accounts, devices, policies, and paths.
03QueueRank what should happen this week and assign owners.
04FixCustomer or MSP makes approved changes outside RoadRunner.
05ValidateNext run checks whether evidence changed.
06ReportLeadership sees verified closure and remaining exposure.
07RepeatRegressions and source gaps feed the next weekly run.

Chapter 01 · Synthetic fixture

Executive posture

Leadership gets risk direction, closure counts, source gaps, and the story of what changed.

Watch for

The composite number is context. The panel that matters is what changed — findings new and resolved, diffed against the previous run, plus the coverage panel that says out loud what cannot be assessed yet.

Chapter 02 · Synthetic fixture

On-prem attack path

The product does not stop at graph visualization. It points to the path edge that should be cut.

Watch for

Click any node and the shortest privilege path to Tier 0 ignites hop by hop. Beside it, the four-hop breakdown and the cheapest defensible edge to cut — with a read-only collector command below.

Chapter 03 · Synthetic fixture

Evidence-grounded vCISO

Answers are grounded in findings and source evidence. Missing data is stated instead of invented.

Watch for

Ask what to fix first and the answer names entities, cites the finding and the source for every claim, and refuses to invent around missing evidence.

The finale

Two weak signals. One provable exposure.

The walkthrough ends the way every week should: combined evidence becomes one artifact with a validation condition — and the next run decides.

Walkthrough finding · RR-F-0161

MFA exception and AD path combine into a priority remediation item

AffectedOne excluded identity, one exposed admin path, one client-visible closure objective
EvidenceMicrosoft policy evidence plus AD collector path evidence create a higher-confidence exposure story.
RiskSingle-domain findings may look manageable alone; combined evidence shows a realistic path to impact.
Recommended actionClose the identity exception and cut the AD path edge before treating either item as resolved.
ValidationNext run must show the policy exception removed and the AD graph path broken.
Closure rule Manual status cannot close this finding. Evidence from the next run must satisfy the validation condition.

Next run decides Manual status cannot close it. That is the whole point.
