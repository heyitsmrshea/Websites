Good questions

Who can attend the camp?

The camp is open to girls entering 8th through 12th grade. Players at every position and every level of experience are welcome — the day includes both instruction and evaluation.

Is registration still open?

Registration for the July 31, 2026 camp is currently full. The waitlist is free to join — if a spot opens up, waitlist families are contacted first.

How much does it cost?

Registration is $300 for the full day, 9:00 AM to 3:00 PM.

What happens during the day?

College coaches are on-site throughout the day for evaluation and instruction. Players get real reps, real coaching, and real looks from programs across NCAA Division I, Division II, NAIA, and junior college softball.

Where exactly is the camp?

Pompano Four Fields, 1701 NE 8th St, Pompano Beach, FL 33060. Open in Google Maps.

Is this camp run by a college?

No. This camp is independently run and is not affiliated with or endorsed by any college or university. Coach attendance is subject to change.
