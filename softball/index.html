what a day here does

Big-league coaching, kid-sized fun

This isn't a tryout with clipboards and silence. It's a full day of playing hard, learning fast, and being watched by people who can open doors.

Get real reps

Skill work and live play all day long, with high-level instruction from coaches who develop college athletes for a living.

College coaches are on-site throughout the day for evaluation — from Division I powerhouses to D2, NAIA, and juco programs.

Her promise for the day: players will be seen, challenged, and guided.
